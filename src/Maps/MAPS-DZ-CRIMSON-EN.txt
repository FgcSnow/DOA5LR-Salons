DOA5LR-Salons - DANGER ZONE + THE CRIMSON 1 & 2 (PS4 stages)
=============================================================

WHAT
- Danger Zone, The Crimson 1 and The Crimson 2 (PS4 versions) are added to DOA5LR PC.
- Pick them by hand in the stage select (proper thumbnails; Crimson 1 and 2 share one slot,
  switch the variant in Solo), offline and online.
- Random: tick them in the game's Random filter (three check boxes). Since 0.3.14 they are drawn
  by OFFLINE Random only; online Random (ranked, lobbies) stays the game's own, so a player
  without the maps never gets them. DOA5LR-RandomStages.ini: Online=1 adds them to online Random
  too - only if everyone you play with has the maps.
- The original stages are all still there.

ONLINE
- EVERYONE in the room (players AND spectators) needs the maps to play on them: update to
  0.3.13 or later before picking them in a room.

INSTALL / REMOVE
- Installer check box "Maps", on by default. Untick it and click Update/Reinstall to leave the
  maps out: their files are removed, your .ini settings are kept. Tick it again to get them back.
- The installer's "Restore backup" also brings back the files from before an update.

AUTOLINK (costume mods)
- Works with the maps. Only put character folders inside AutoLink (AYANE, KASUMI...) plus
  _Movie, _Texture, _Sound, _Stages. Modding tools stored in AutoLink (_Modding) made a tester's
  game crash after the first fight; after moving them out, no more crashes.

IF IT CRASHES OR FREEZES
- Open DOA5LR-Diagnostic\DOA5LR Diagnostic.cmd and click "Send my logs" (if the game is frozen,
  click it BEFORE closing the game). You see the content of the ZIP; nothing is sent unless you
  confirm. Keep the ".dmp" box ticked: it shows where the game crashed.
- "Play (with the probe)" starts the game with a small read-only observer that records the stage,
  fight and how the game exited. "Detailed crash reports" (optional, admin rights once) makes
  Windows keep a .dmp when the game crashes.
- Never sent: IP, SteamID, chat, controller inputs, saves. The .dmp holds a piece of the game's
  memory and may include your Steam name (untick it if you prefer).

FILES
- DOA5LR-DangerZone / DNZ-* / Crimson* / ExtraStages / RandomStages / DebugArchive .asi (+ .ini)
  next to game.exe, stage data in CodexDangerZone, CodexCrimson and PS4Stages.
- These modules write local logs next to the game (DOA5LR-Crimson-VFX.log, ...). Since 0.3.14 the
  Crimson effects log only records startup and errors, capped at 512 KB ([Log] in
  DOA5LR-Crimson-VFX.ini: Level=0 off, Level=2 every effect, MaxKB = cap).
