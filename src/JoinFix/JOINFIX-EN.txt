DOA5LR-JoinFix 0.3 (pack 0.3.10)
================================

Fixes rooms that nobody can join.

The problem
-----------
When you create a room, DOA5LR puts a random network encryption key (24 bytes) in the Steam room
data, but it stores it as text. If one random byte is 0, Steam cuts the key there. Players who join
get a broken key: they enter the Steam room, the network link works, but the host's game ignores
them and they leave after 30 seconds. It happens in about 1 room in 11, at random, and a broken room
stays broken for EVERYONE until it is closed and created again.

What JoinFix does
-----------------
- KeyFix (main fix): just before YOUR room is published, zero bytes of the key are replaced in the
  game's own key and in the published copy. The game reads the key again for every packet, so
  everything stays consistent. When the key has no zero byte (10 rooms in 11), nothing is changed.
  It works on the HOST side: everyone who creates rooms needs the pack 0.3.10 or newer.
- AcceptMembers: always accepts the network link of players who are in your room.
- InviteRetry: after you accept an invite, asks Steam again for the room data if it is missing.
- F7 = copy room link: in a room, press F7 to copy its steam://joinlobby/... link to the clipboard
  (high beep). Paste it on Discord: a click on it joins your room (game already running). Private
  rooms have no "Join game" button in Steam, this replaces it. Key set by CopyLinkKey in the .ini.
- FastFail (off by default): stops a join at once, with the game's own error message, when Steam
  reports that the host cannot be reached (instead of waiting up to 90 s).

Settings: scripts\DOA5LR-JoinFix.ini (1 = on, 0 = off). No log file, nothing is sent anywhere.
Source: scripts\JoinFix-Source (build.cmd, LLVM-MinGW 32-bit). Found and validated on 25/09/2026.
